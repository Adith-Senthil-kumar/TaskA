The point of view
Calm by default. Orange only when the phone owes the server something.
A bright, quiet, native-feeling app. No permanent warning chrome — one status card at the top of the route says the honest thing, and a small orange “1 to send” pill appears only on rows that haven’t reached the server.
Rules
Offline is neutral gray — a fact, not an error. Sync-failed is the only red. Conflicts are purple and rare. Green appears exactly twice: shift progress, and the all-caught-up moment.
Synced work shows nothing at all on the route — absence of the orange pill is the “done” state, so the list stays readable at arm’s length.
Where I diverged
Screen 4 is split: Outbox (queue, sync, conflicts) and Profile (driver, van, sign-out). Conflicts resolve on the order itself, with the address and history in view.
