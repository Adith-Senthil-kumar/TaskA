The point of view
Synced is quiet. Unsent is loud.
Most apps celebrate success with colour. Here, colour means the phone still owes the server something. When the screen goes calm and grey, the work has landed. A driver learns that in one shift and never has to read a word again.
Rules I set
The square chip never relies on colour alone — outline style and fill carry the same message, so it survives rain, glare and colour-blindness.
The truth strip under the status bar is on every screen. The app is never allowed to imply something is sent when it isn’t.
Offline is a neutral fact, not an error. Sync-failed is the only red state, because it is the only one the driver might eventually need to act on.
Where I diverged
Screen 4 is split in two: Outbox (the queue, sync, conflicts) and Profile (driver, van, sign-out). Sync is checked mid-shift, settings almost never — putting them together buries the thing that matters.
Conflicts resolve on the order detail, not in a separate screen. The decision needs the address, the items and the history next to it.
Try
Go offline → open a stop → Update status → save. Then Outbox → Sync now and watch the chips settle.
